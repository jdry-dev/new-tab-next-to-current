# Privacy Policy: New Tab Next To Current

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

New Tab Next To Current is a Chrome extension that moves newly opened tabs next to the currently active tab.

## Data collection

The extension does **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal or browsing data. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

## What the extension accesses, and why

The extension requests no permissions. It cannot see the address, title or content of any tab, or your browsing history. When a tab is created, it uses only the tab's position, whether it is active, and which tab opened it, so it can move the new tab next to that tab. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
