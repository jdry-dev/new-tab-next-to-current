## Store listing tab

**Name** (from manifest): New Tab Next To Current

**Summary** (from manifest `description`, max 132 chars):
Opens new tabs (Ctrl+T) right next to the currently active tab instead of at the end of the tab strip.

**Description:**

```
Tired of Ctrl+T throwing your new tab all the way to the end of a long tab strip?

New Tab Next To Current opens every new tab directly to the right of the tab you are working in, so related tabs stay together and you never have to hunt for the tab you just opened.

How it works
• Press Ctrl+T (Cmd+T on Mac) or click the "+" button: the new tab appears right next to your current tab.
• Tabs opened in the background (middle-click, Ctrl+click) are left alone.
• No settings, no toolbar button: install it and it just works.

Privacy
• Requires no permissions: it cannot see the pages you visit or your browsing history.
• No data is collected, stored, or sent anywhere.

Notes
• If the current tab is pinned, the new tab opens at the end of the strip as usual (Chrome does not allow unpinned tabs between pinned ones).
```
